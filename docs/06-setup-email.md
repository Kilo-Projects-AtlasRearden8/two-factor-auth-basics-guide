# Step-by-Step: Email

Your email is the most important account to protect. Password resets for almost everything else go to your email, so if someone gets into your email, they can get into everything else.

Protect your primary email first.

---

## Gmail (Google)

1. Sign in to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com.
2. Click **Security** in the left menu (or the top tab).
3. Under "How you sign in to Google," click **2-Step Verification**. You may need to sign in again.
4. Click **Get started**.
5. Follow the prompts. Google will offer to send a text or call — choose that if you want, then look for **"Authenticator app"** or "Set up an authenticator app" as an option.
6. Choose **Authenticator app**, pick your phone type (Android or iPhone), and scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
7. Enter the six-digit code the app shows to confirm it works.
8. **Save your backup codes** — Google calls them "Backup codes." Click "Continue," then download or print them and store them safely.
9. Turn 2-Step Verification on.

## Outlook / Hotmail / Microsoft 365

1. Sign in to account.microsoft.com.
2. Click **Security** → **Advanced security options**.
3. Under "Additional security," find **Two-step verification** and click **Turn on**.
4. Follow the prompts to add a verification method.
5. Choose **An app** (Microsoft Authenticator or another authenticator app).
6. Scan the QR code or enter the setup key manually.
7. Enter the code from the app to finish.
8. **Save your recovery code** — Microsoft gives you a recovery code. Print it or write it down.

## Yahoo Mail

1. Sign in to your Yahoo account and go to Account Security.
2. Find **Two-step verification** and toggle it on.
3. Choose your method. Select an authenticator app if offered, otherwise SMS is better than nothing.
4. Follow the prompts to scan the QR code or enter the setup key.
5. **Save any backup codes** Yahoo provides.

## Apple ID / iCloud Mail

1. Go to appleid.apple.com and sign in, or on your iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → Password & Security.
2. Turn on **Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Apple will ask for a trusted phone number to receive codes.
4. Apple doesn't use a standard authenticator app — it sends codes to your trusted devices. Keep your trusted phone number current and your devices signed in.
5. Set up a **recovery key** if Apple offers it, and store it safely.

## General tips for email

- Turn on 2FA on your *primary* email first, then your backup email if you have one.
- After turning on 2FA, some apps (like mail apps on your phone) may need an "app password." The website will walk you through this if needed.
- Make sure your recovery phone number and recovery email are up to date.
- Save your backup/recovery codes — print them out.
