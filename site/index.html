Two-Factor Authentication Basics

Keep your accounts safe — even if someone steals your password.

Start here

What this guide is for

You have accounts — email, banking, shopping, AI tools, social media. Each one is a door, and your password is the key. The problem is keys get copied, guessed, or stolen all the time.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second lock to the door. Even if someone learns your password, they still can’t get in without a second piece of proof that it’s really you.

What’s inside

  • Authenticator apps — the best everyday choice for 2FA
  • SMS risks — why text-message codes are the weakest option
  • Recovery codes — your emergency spare key
  • Backing up your accounts — so you don’t get locked out
  • Step-by-step setup: email
  • Step-by-step setup: banking
  • Step-by-step setup: AI tools
  • Printable worksheet — write it all down safely

Who this is for

Anyone with an email address or a bank account. You don’t need to be a programmer. You don’t need to spend money. You do need about an hour and a notebook.

This guide is education only. It’s about protecting your accounts — it is not medical, financial, or legal advice.
